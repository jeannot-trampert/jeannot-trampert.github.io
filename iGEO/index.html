Integrated geodynamics (iGEO): Reconciling geophysics and geochemistry

This 5 year project started in 2013 and aims to reconcile the views of geophysics and geochemistry on the Earth's deep mantle

Project Summary

Read more

How are deep mantle processes related to the mapped geological record? How can we reconcile geochemical observations with geophysical inferences?

These are first order unanswered questions despite our steady progress in imaging the Earth's internal structure and understanding the high temperature and pressure properties of minerals.
